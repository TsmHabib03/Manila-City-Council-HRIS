Year-End Mandatory/Forced Leave Processing

At the end of each year every employee's leave balance is reduced by the unused portion of the five (5) mandatory/forced leave days. Employees who already used part of their mandatory leave are only deducted the remainder; employees who used all five days are not deducted. The run is safe to repeat — employees already processed for the year are skipped. It also runs automatically every December 31.
